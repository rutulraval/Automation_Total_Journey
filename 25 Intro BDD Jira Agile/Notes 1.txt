BDD
=========

A product owner gives us requirements and it tells us that our app should have this feature in a given scenario and it should behave in a certain way.

All these instructions are in plain and simple English, in a layman's term.

FORMAT OF REQUIREMENTS SHOULD BE : 
---------------------------
Given....when....then


(Here "then" represents acceptance criteria)

Now, Product Owner should ideally give those requirements to a business analyst.
BA will understand those requirements, and prepare an EPIC, which eventually will be divided into Features.

-A Feature will be divided into user stories.
(User stories are well defined criteria 
 in the format: Given....when....then)
  If all the acceptance criteria are fulfilled, then a particular User Story is called finished.


So, crux of the flow is:

EPIC--->FEATURE-->USER STORIES

And this is called Behaviour Driven Development in Agile environment.

Backlog:
----------
When all USER STORIES(requirements), FEATURES are identified by a BA or Product Owner, are stored in "Backlog" in JIRA or Confluence.(These tools are developed by Atlassian)


CEREMONY
----------
Backlog grooming is a ceremony in Agile, where all team members sit together and discuss what all details our User Story/Feature should have.



Pulling story from BACKLOG to Current Sprint
=============================================
Product Owner will decide based on priority whether a certain story should be developed/worked upon in current Sprint or not.


JIRA is a licensed product.
It is used to document all project related events and maintain history.



50:00
--------
All the technical details can't be mentioned on JIRA, it is not practical.

For technical details of any project/feature/user story are stored on CONFLUENCE.


AGILE is an approach of Software Development Life Cycle.

Both BDD and TDD can be handled in AGILE way.
